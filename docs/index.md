# nx-wiki

Welcome to my personal knowledge base: notes, how-tos, snippets, references and ideas, all in one place.

Use the search bar at the top to find anything, or browse the sections in the navigation.

## Sections

- [Getting started](getting-started/index.md): how this wiki is organised and how to add to it
- [Mechanics](mechanics/index.md): classical, continuum and fluid mechanics
- [Networking](networking/index.md): network layers, protocols and how devices talk to each other

## Reading offline

**On your phone:** install the wiki as an app. In Chrome on Android, tap **⋮ → Install app**; on iPhone, tap **Share → Add to Home Screen** in Safari. The whole wiki is saved on the device and works without internet.

**On a computer:** Download [nx-wiki-offline.zip](https://serjhnn.github.io/nx-wiki/nx-wiki-offline.zip), unzip it and open `nx-wiki/index.html` in a browser. Everything works without internet: search, math, diagrams and fonts. The download is rebuilt every time the wiki changes.
